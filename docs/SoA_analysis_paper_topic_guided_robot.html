15 Kulkarni et al., 2019 Review of Robotic Needle Guide Systems for Percutaneous Intervention Annals of Biomedical Engineering Systematic review
2008–2019, ~80 robotic systems
Categories: MRI / CT / US / multi-imager		 All major robots (MrBot, KUKA MED, UR series, various custom) — comparative review across ~80 systems All actuator types reviewed: piezoelectric, pneumatic, hydraulic, DC, ultrasonic, tendon-driven. Compatibility tests (SNR, homogeneity, distortion) reviewed per modality. All control schemes reviewed: PID, SMC, PI, TDC, NNPID, impedance, co-manipulation (free/locked mode). No single control method proposed — taxonomy of existing approaches. FDA/regulatory: only manual needle insertion approved; robot limited to guide positioning
MRI-guided: non-magnetic materials mandatory (PEEK, nylon, Delrin, ABS, polyoxymethylene)
US-guided: least constrained modality — DC motors applicable, any structural material
CT-guided: X-ray transparency required; carbon fibre, PEEK, ceramics used
Trend: body-attached systems, multi-imager compatibility, combined force + image feedback
~80 systems reviewed; most for prostate and breast; few for liver and neurosurgery Essential reading before writing SoA chapter — provides taxonomy, terminology, and regulatory context for the whole field. 		10.1007/s10439-019-02319-9
